# Supplementary Materials
This repository provides the supplementary materials for the following article:

**A rapid hazard-to-network framework for assessing earthquake-induced transport disruption and road-network connectivity**

The supplementary materials are provided as an external companion to the article and offer additional methodological details and parameter information supporting the event-conditioned hazard-to-network framework and its application to the 2025 Myanmar earthquake. This repository provides open, version-controlled access to the supporting information referenced in the manuscript.

## Contents

The supplementary appendix is organized into three interconnected sections corresponding to the principal physical components of the framework:

1. **Ground-motion parameters and associated analyses**
   This section presents the earthquake-source information, ground-motion records, ground-motion prediction equation implementation, intensity-measure parameters, and related analyses used to construct the event-specific shaking field. The corresponding parameter information is summarized in **Table S1**.

2. **Bridge parameters and associated analyses**
   This section describes the bridge inventory, structural and fragility parameters, seismic-response assessment, and translation of bridge damage into operational closure states for road-network analysis. The corresponding bridge parameters are summarized in **Table S2**.

3. **Geological parameters and coseismic-landslide analyses**
   This section presents the geological and terrain inputs, Newmark-based displacement analysis, coseismic-landslide assessment, and spatial identification of landslide-induced road-blockage locations. The corresponding geological and landslide-analysis parameters are summarized in **Table S3**.

Together, these materials document the parameterization and analytical procedures used to derive the common ground-motion field, bridge-closure states, landslide-blockage states, and their subsequent integration into the road-network assessment.
